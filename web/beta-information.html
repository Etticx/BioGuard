About this beta.

This is a private test. Management reviews sightings and assigns an ecology field team. Removal and replanting need management approval.


Identification photos are processed temporarily. Drafts keep local photo copies. Submitted reports store photos, staff details, location, notes, model suggestions and review history for authorized staff.


Coordinates are optional. Test photos are not automatically used for training. The model covers nine species and may be wrong even at high confidence.


Use test accounts. Avoid photos of people or private documents. Ask the beta organizer to correct or remove submitted test data. Removing a draft does not remove a submitted report.
